# Brooks Law

Use when a deadline slips and adding people is proposed.

Prose: gain O(N), coordination O(N2), plus ramp-up per Mythical Man-Month. Prefer scope cuts and critical-path protection. Time-box any onboarding off the critical path.

Logic:
- require deadline, critical path, onboarding cost exists
- estimate net gain or loss of adding staff
- propose scope cut or pair-support instead
- return decision plus protected path
