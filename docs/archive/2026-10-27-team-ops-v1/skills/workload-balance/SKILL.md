# Workload Balance

Use when distributing work across people or squads.

Prose: Taylor for repeatable tasks only; Belbin 9 roles for squad mix, ideal 6; Dunbar split past 9. Cap WIP 1-2 per dev. Cover forming, storming, norming, performing; protect psychological safety. Match skill tags to task, balance load.

Logic:
- require capacity and skill tags exists
- assign by fit, cap WIP, keep squads small
- flag overload or skill gaps
- return assignments plus risks
