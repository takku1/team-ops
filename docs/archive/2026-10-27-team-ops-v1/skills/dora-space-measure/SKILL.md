# DORA SPACE Measure

Use when productivity claims need evidence.

Prose: DORA covers deployment frequency, lead time, failure rate, restore time. SPACE adds satisfaction, performance, activity, communication, efficiency. Measure at team level only, across at least three SPACE dimensions. Never rank individuals by these numbers.

Logic:
- require team scope and period exists
- collect DORA four plus three SPACE signals
- flag vanity metrics like lines of code
- return health snapshot plus friction to remove
