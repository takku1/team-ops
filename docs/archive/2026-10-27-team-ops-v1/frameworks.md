# Frameworks research (applied, not academic survey)

Sources (web, 2026-10-27): MIT/Harvard mirroring-hypothesis work on Conway via Nextage/Jama; Jargon File plus Brooks OS/360 account for Brooks O(N) gain vs O(N2) coordination; Atlassian Kanban-vs-Scrum for cadence plus WIP; Taskade/WalkMe/PMBOK-glossary for RACI plus WBS; Belbin 9-role balance research.

- **Conway's Law (1967):** communication structure copies into design. Evidence: distributed teams ship more modular code. Inverse move: design team boundaries first (Team Topologies). Use: one team per service, fix collaboration before code.
- **Brooks's Law:** late plus staff equals later. Gain O(N), coordination O(N2), plus ramp-up. Use: freeze onboarding onto critical path, time-box help, cut scope first.
- **Taylorism:** specialize, measure, standardize repeatable tasks. Limit: harms creative work. Use for checklists only.
- **Agile/Scrum:** Product Owner, Scrum Master, Developers; Sprint Planning, Daily, Review, Retro. Sprint Backlog is implicit WIP limit. Use for uncertain scope.
- **Kanban:** explicit WIP per column, flow metrics lead time, cycle time, throughput. Replenish as capacity opens. Use for support flow.
- **WBS (PMBOK):** hierarchical split to work packages with cost plus done test. Feeds RACI input.
- **RACI:** Responsible does, one Accountable owns, Consulted gives input, Informed gets updates. Variants RASCI, DACI. One A per task.
- **Belbin 9 roles:** Coordinator, Shaper, Plant, Monitor, Specialist, Implementer, Finisher, Team Worker, Resource Investigator. Ideal small squad is 6; cover Coordinator plus Plant plus Implementer.
- **Dunbar:** stable network near 150; delivery squads 5-9, split past 9.
- **Workload balancing:** assign by capacity plus skill tags, WIP 1-2 per dev, Jira or Trello board Backlog-ToDo-Doing-Review-Done. Cover Tuckman stages plus safety.
- **Team Topologies:** stream-aligned, platform, enabling, complicated-subsystem; modes collaboration, X-as-a-service, facilitation. Reduce load on stream teams.
- **DORA plus SPACE:** DORA four plus SPACE five across three dims min, team level only.
- **Estimation guardrails:** Hofstadter plus planning fallacy, Parkinson, Goodhart. Use reference class plus buffers.
- **Constraint flow:** one bottleneck at a time, subordinate the rest.

Best-value synthesis: Conway plus Topologies set boundaries, WBS splits work, RACI assigns ownership, Scrum or Kanban runs flow, Brooks plus estimation plus WIP limits protect dates, DORA plus SPACE proves health.
