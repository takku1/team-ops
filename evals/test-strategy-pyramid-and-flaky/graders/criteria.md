Prioritizes escaped billing defects and meaningful assertions. Chooses test layers by risk and speed, not a mandatory 70/20/10 count. Adds contract coverage alongside real billing integration semantics. Removes the assertion-free 100% gate; keeps coverage diagnostic. Assigns flaky tests an owner, deadline, visible retry/quarantine status, and compensating coverage before removing critical protection. Fails for silent retries, ownerless flakes, or deleting the only safety check because two weeks elapsed.
