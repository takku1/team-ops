Picks a lightweight set of phases (implement and verify, perhaps a short
inline review) and runs or plans them inline, with an explicit
acceptance check for each slice. Does not spawn or propose a full roster
of subagents, write policy or repo-brief files nobody asked for, require
production SLOs, or fabricate deployment history. Missing history is
unknown, not a reason to block planning.

Grade observed decisions and evidence, not exact wording. A proposed run is not an executed run.
