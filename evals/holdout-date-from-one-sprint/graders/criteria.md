A successful answer gives an honest range rather than a single 90% date from one data point: it explains that one sprint cannot support a calibrated 90% percentile, notes ticket-size variation and scope change, offers a range (e.g. naive 5 sprints with wide uncertainty) labeled as uncalibrated, and says what data would justify a percentile (more throughput history, a simulation). It names risks and a re-forecast point. Fails if it presents a specific 90%-confidence date as calibrated.
