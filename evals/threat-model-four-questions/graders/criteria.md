A successful answer follows Shostack's four questions (what are we working on,
what can go wrong, what are we going to do about it, did we do a good job),
models the system with trust boundaries, and raises at least: background
clipboard snooping, focus spoofing or focus-steal to read a paste, the shell
as a confused deputy for paste commands, and sensitive clips lingering
(expiry/exclusion for password-manager clips). Each threat gets a mitigation.
Fails if it is a generic list without the four-question structure.
